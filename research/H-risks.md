# H · Risks and open questions

Severity reflects impact on CertCity's product promise or viable launch. Likelihood is a qualitative judgment based on the present evidence, before mitigation. Rank prioritizes early resolution over precision. [Decision register](decision-register.md)

| Rank | Risk | Severity / likelihood | Mitigation, owner and reversal signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Players master visual cues and miss the same distinction in a technical case. | Critical / High | Learning lead runs delayed, unfamiliar assessments against an active comparator. Add fading, contrasting cases and actual traces. If the gap persists, simplify the visual mechanic or use a technical micro-lab where needed. [Butler, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20804289/), [Fyfe et al., 2014](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1036777) |
| 2 | The roleplay feels like selecting quiz answers in a slow interface. | High / High | Game lead tests investigating, editing, replay and revision in one room. Measure time-to-first-action and voluntary replay. If world navigation slows the work, shorten the path to a job and provide a resume shortcut from the city. [Mini Metro](https://store.steampowered.com/app/287980/Mini_Metro/), [Sundararajan and Adesope, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09522-4) |
| 3 | Exam eligibility limits acquisition, and free official prep wins. | Critical / High | Product lead tests paid partner cohorts, direct eligibility checks and a public skills demo. If partner demand is absent, validate broader practical training or a more accessible credential before investing in full CCAR-F coverage. [Anthropic program](https://claude.com/resources/articles/four-role-based-claude-certifications), [Academy catalog](https://academy.claude.com/courses) |
| 4 | Objective coverage, item validity and release drift undermine trust. | Critical / High | Curriculum lead maintains versioned bullet-level evidence, exact runtime fixture versions and two-person content review. Recheck the guide before each release and flag revised objectives. [CCAR-F guide](https://github.com/Amey-Thakur/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/blob/main/architect-foundations/exam-guide.pdf), [Claude Code releases](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/releases) |
| 5 | Readiness gives false precision or is used for hiring. | Critical / Medium | Measurement owner withholds an aggregate under sparse evidence, retains family exposure and versions, and audits score interpretation and fairness. Require independent criterion validation before pass likelihood or employment-facing use. [Testing Standards](https://www.apa.org/science/programs/testing/standards) |
| 6 | Art throughput or mobile limitations break the visual promise. | High / Medium–High | Art and frontend leads approve one finished cutaway, validate mobile readability and benchmark same-art sprites against alternatives. Reuse modular props and reduce idle animation before scaling the district. [Phaser renderer](https://phaser.io/news/2026/04/phaser-4-renderer-faster-cleaner-and-built-for-modern-games), [Blender LTS](https://www.blender.org/releases/4-5/) |
| 7 | Simulated hours become misleading claims of cash, staffing or real business impact. | High / Medium | Simulation owner keeps a visible assumption ledger and tests cost/replay invariants. Render fixed payroll, avoided overtime and hypothetical capacity separately. If economics distract or depend on unjustified assumptions, focus on reliable outcomes and minutes. [SimPy](https://simpy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), [B · Economic model](B-game-design.md) |
| 8 | NPC dialogue hallucinates facts, misstates a rule, or leaks assessment data. | Critical / Medium | AI owner limits the facts and inputs by role, isolates held-out items, uses authored fallback and evaluates contradictory and sensitive prompts. Ship optional generation only on measured benefit with no critical acceptance failures. [Anthropic agent patterns](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents), [Structured outputs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/structured-outputs) |
| 9 | Different scene and server states or retries corrupt learning history. | High / Medium | Engineering owner pins versions, commits with short transactions and tests replay, races and idempotency. Disable grading when an authoritative result cannot be recovered and resume the attempt safely. [Postgres locking](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/explicit-locking.html), [E · Architecture](E-technical-architecture.md) |
| 10 | Content and support costs erase early margins or create ongoing rework. | High / Medium–High | Product and content leads track actual time per reviewed objective and cohort. Use one paid cohort and one second-pack reuse experiment. Avoid subscription forecasts without repeat usage. [Claude pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing), [CertGrove pricing](https://certgrove.com/pricing) |
| 11 | FarmVille inspiration or certification marketing creates rights or affiliation problems. | High / Medium | Counsel reviews name, visuals, sales copy, policy and licenses. Use original authored examples, independent branding and a provenance register. Resolve scope before external marketing. [U.S. Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-games.html), [USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion), [Certification policy inventory](https://github.com/Amey-Thakur/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/blob/main/guide/official-sources.md) |
| 12 | Canvas, motion or technical jargon makes the experience inaccessible. | High / Medium | Design and QA owners provide DOM action parity, non-drag alternatives, high contrast, pause and reduced motion. Include target users with access needs in testing. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) |
| 13 | Tenant, learner or study data become visible beyond intended scope. | Critical / Low–Medium | Engineering and privacy owners enforce auth, RLS, separate evidence privileges, retention and consent. Test second-tenant access and restricted provider data flows before pilot. [Supabase RLS](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security), [Anthropic Legal](https://www.anthropic.com/legal) |
| 14 | A reusable platform abstraction slows the first mission and bakes in the wrong assumptions. | Medium / High | Tech lead reuses versioned contracts and a few mechanics. Introduce a new abstraction only after a second scenario or certification makes the shared need concrete. [CCAR-F guide](https://github.com/Amey-Thakur/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/blob/main/architect-foundations/exam-guide.pdf), [1EdTech QTI](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/qti) |

## Questions that could change the recommendation

| Question | Fastest credible evidence |
|---|---|
| Does finding a job in the city improve context or slow the first decision? | Compare city exploration and direct mission entry using the same cases and prompts. Observe action time, replay explanation and voluntary next job. |
| Can players apply the lesson when the visual metaphor is gone? | Run the delayed study and a second form where the same message masks different source evidence. Test recognition and rationale independently. |
| What does live dialogue add beyond carefully written stakeholder responses? | Randomize an authored-card versus bounded-AI cohort within the same mission. Compare essential facts found, reasoning, abandonment, accuracy and actual usage. |
| Is a sprite-first scene art-directable at the required quality and cost? | Produce one comparable approved cutaway in 2D-baked and live 3D approaches. Compare artist hours, occlusion, interaction clarity and device results. |
| Which training buyer will pay while official courses are free? | Test paid partner pilots with real eligible learners and track procurement friction, usage after the first session and support time. |
| How much scope changes in adjacent Claude credentials? | Recheck official portal and revised guides after the reported publication date, then author one reviewed mission reusing an institution. |
| Is a readiness index useful at all? | Test interpretation of evidence counts, stale evidence and uncertainty with learners and cohort leads. Revisit the numeric headline only after validity and utility evidence. |
