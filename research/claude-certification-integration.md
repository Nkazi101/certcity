## Decisions and evidence

| Decision | Evidence and application |
|---|---|
| Keep one catalog and a distinct pathway for each role and level. | The official credential pages define different candidate profiles, exam weights and outcomes. Show role, level and exam code at selection. Put registration, partner eligibility and official materials beside the relevant pathway. [Anthropic’s four credentials](https://claude.com/resources/articles/four-role-based-claude-certifications) · [Partner Academy FAQ](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications) |
| Put case evidence next to the decision and replay the consequences. | Simulations can support complex skills when guidance and feedback are built in. All 34 assignments open inside their associated building from Certifications, Assignments, or the map. Each room has a location-specific interior and two authored stakeholder conversations; each reply is tied to the mission case. Learners inspect evidence, configure the relevant system, replay it, then read an explicit technical debrief and complete the mapped exam-style check. The city retains successful outcomes at each place and in City impact. Maplewood School replays per-message routing, Juniper Kitchen recalculates invoice totals and applies the selected posting policy, and Northline Systems checks candidate and guardrail choices against three pilot thresholds. City Dispatch retains its purpose-built cutaway, editable workflow, and replay. New job outputs are deterministic and use synthetic fixtures. Simulation research supports scaffolding and feedback as design elements; CertCity still needs learner evaluation before claiming learning impact. [Chernikova et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654320933544) · [Alfieri et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0021017) · [CertCity workflow research](workflow-usability.md) · [CertCity validation plan](G-mvp-plan.md) |
| Write original decisions with one defensible key and plausible alternatives. | All items identify a concrete job, an observation or constraint that changes the decision, the number of required picks, and an explanation for each option. Single-response items have four choices and select-two items have five. Each rationale identifies the particular evidence that makes an option fit or fail. The practice is static and deterministic. Public guides supplied coverage and format, and technical documentation supports the keys. [NBME item-writing guidance](https://www2.nbme.org/nbme/IWTutorial/eIWW_3/index.html) · [CertCity practice audit](exam-practice-audit.md) |
| Offer mixed and domain practice. | A 12-item mixed session reserves one place for each domain, distributes the remaining places toward the largest shortfalls against exam weights, and prefers unanswered questions inside each domain. This gives breadth with a manageable session length. Domain practice uses all available items for the selected domain. CertCity’s optional countdown approximates average official pacing. Sessions are practice sets of original vignettes. The official CCAR-F guide describes a longer grouped scenario exam. [CCAR-F guide, §§3–6](https://cc.sj-cdn.net/instructor/6nizmqk8tpzpfjvt6qmmav7rh/public/1794946250/Claude_Certified_Architect_-_Foundations_Exam_Guide.pdf) · [Brunmair and Richter, 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31556629/) |
| Reveal answer explanations after submission and offer a new follow-up set. | Choices, rationale and source appear together in review. Each mission check schedules a held-out question for every represented exam domain, available seven days after the check is submitted. It is labeled as domain-level practice and is not counted as objective coverage. The interval follows CertCity’s pilot design. Research supports studying retrieval over a delay, with the best interval depending on the target retention period. [Shute, 2008](https://myweb.fsu.edu/vshute/pdf/shute%202008_b.pdf) · [Butler, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20804289/) · [Cepeda et al., 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19076480/) |
| Report raw scores and the amount of evidence behind each domain. | Review reports the exact number correct. The pathway counts unique completed questions and uses the first completed response per item for domain evidence. Repeated answers appear in history. Held-out results are reported in their own session. There is no empirical conversion to Anthropic’s scaled cut score or certification likelihood. [Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing](https://www.apa.org/science/programs/testing/standards) · [CertCity decision register](decision-register.md) |
| Reuse the established City Dispatch module and conversation pattern. | The existing CertificationDialog is the pathway shell for every building assignment: four progress rows, a current action, optional practice, and official guide/pathway links. Starting the assignment enters the relevant building and uses the existing ConversationFrame for stakeholder dialogue; case evidence, system choices, and replay stay within that interaction. The debrief, original exam-style check, and dated held-out follow-up return to the same pathway shell. This follows the user-provided City Dispatch reference and removes the separate tabbed workbench approach. Native radio buttons, checkboxes and details support keyboard interaction; text exposes answer state, visible focus and section headings support navigation, and timing is opt-in. | Use the app’s static export and browser-local progress. | Each credential has a versioned browser-local practice key. The city saves the chosen rules for completed jobs independently of practice, and remembers the most recent job. A saved practice draft contains question IDs, selections, position and optional deadline. Submission uses the latest saved answers and is idempotent by session ID. The city’s original keys still preserve existing results. ## Practice benchmark

Each available guide has two timed forms. The benchmark uses complete-form results with recorded exposure and zero previously seen items. Questions Anthropic’s format treats as unscored are excluded. CertCity uses 80% correct overall and at least 67% rounded in every domain. The independent practical and its separate seven-day case each require every structured check to pass. The dashboard also reports first-attempt criterion evidence on practical cases. Revisions are allowed. The thresholds are CertCity design choices. An assessment of their ability to predict exam results awaits independent learner outcomes.

The short starting checks give actionable feedback before entering a city assignment. The capstone produces a role-specific artifact and checks it against synthetic scenarios. The later case changes the scenario and fixtures. The preparation view reports guided work, city application, timed results and practical criteria by the selected guide. Earlier results remain with their own guide and previously seen questions remain review evidence.
