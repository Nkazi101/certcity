# Research-to-decision register

**Reviewed November 12, 2026.** Confidence refers to the fit of the proposal for the first slice, given the available evidence. High confidence in a source fact does not establish effectiveness in CertCity. Proposed targets and commercial choices are hypotheses.

| Decision | Research evidence | Alternatives | Rationale | Confidence | Validation needed |

| Coverage-first reporting and cautious internal index | [Testing Standards](https://www.apa.org/science/programs/testing/standards), [CCAR-F scaling](https://github.com/Amey-Thakur/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/blob/main/architect-foundations/exam-guide.pdf), [Mislevy, 1994](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/psychometrika/article/abs/evidence-and-inference-in-educational-assessment/592B899A2036FE7CDD48F2B0880E6F82) | Single readiness number, immediate IRT, official cut-score mapping | Sparse and dependent observations need visible uncertainty. | Codex and simple spaced recommendations | [Cepeda et al., 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19076480/), [Brunmair and Richter, 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31556629/) | Endless adaptive question feed, universal daily schedule | Brief concept references and targeted delayed checks fit an episodic study goal. | Medium | Test intervals and recommendation value with retention data. |
