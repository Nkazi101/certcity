# CCAR-F practice format audit

Reviewed November 12, 2026. Anthropic’s July 2026 v1.0 guide describes a 60-item, 120-minute exam with four of six production scenarios. Each scenario frames several questions, and each question specifies how many responses to select. Section 9’s public examples show four-option single-response items, decisions constrained by evidence from the scenario, and explanations that address the distractors. Anthropic’s FAQ says the former practice exam was retired during the Pearson migration and points candidates to the guide samples. The guide and FAQ govern scope and format. CertCity now uses an original shared production scenario per check, four choices on single-response items and five on select-two items. Similar-looking options differ on concrete constraints such as an upstream timeout versus an empty response, a fixed deadline, an authorization boundary or the side effects of a retry. One question is shown at a time. Players can revisit responses before submission. Scoring requires the exact set of responses for each item and explanations appear after submission. This scoring rule is for CertCity’s practice checks. The public guide is the source for the official scaled score and format. Earlier prototype results remain in practice history with their original score.

Each check covers one objective. The four preparation steps in the UI measure completion of this module, and the coverage label separately identifies how much of the 30-objective blueprint is available. The practice-bank and passer reports support format and item-writing choices. Matching live difficulty and predicting readiness require reviewed items and learner evidence. The seven-day follow-up follows the [MVP evidence plan](G-mvp-plan.md).
